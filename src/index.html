    • SYM HD2 HD200i Motorcycle

    Review

    The SYM HD2 HD200i is a flat floor board scooter motorcycle with 16 inch wheels, a 170cc 4 stroke liquid cooled 4 stroke single cylinder engine and a cvt transmission.

    It has a relatively tall seat height and weighs around 300 lbs wet, and is not suitable for people who are shorter than 5 ft 6.

    The HD200i has a top speed of 75mph with a single person and 65mph with a passenger. The fuel economy of the HD200i is marketed around 80mph, however, its real life fuel economy is 65-70mpg.

    The HD200i has a lot of get up and go and is very easy to ride, however, due to its displacement, it can feel a little underpowered on the interstate.

    The HD200i does not have much storage space, its front glove box is taken up by the coolant resevoir, and its underseat storage space is only big enough to fit a small full face helmet. Overall, the HD200i does not have much storage space. For more storage space, you can purchase a top case.

    Features

    Sleek

    The HD200i has a sleek and modern design with 16 inch wheels.

    Power

    The HD200i uses a 171cc 4 stroke liquid cooled engine with ceramic coating.

    Comfort

    The HD200i has a flat floor board and a comfortable seat for long rides.
